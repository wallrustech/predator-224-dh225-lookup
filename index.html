Maintenance checklist

  • Check engine oil before each use.
  • Keep the air filter clean and correctly installed.
  • Inspect the spark plug and gap periodically.
  • Inspect fuel lines, tank and carburetor for leaks.
  • Keep cooling fins and the engine area free of debris.
  • Record oil changes, plug changes, carburetor adjustments and repairs.

Quick troubleshooting

Won't start
Check fuel, fuel valve, ignition/kill circuit, oil-level protection, spark and choke.
Starts then dies
Check fuel flow, stale fuel, carburetor, air filter and tank venting.
Runs rough
On this modified engine, check VM22 jetting, mixture setting, intake leaks, spark plug, fuel quality and governor/throttle linkage.
Throttle does not respond correctly
Inspect the throttle assembly, cable travel, governor linkage and return springs before operating.
Fuel leak
Stop the engine. Inspect the tank, fuel valve, line, fittings and VM22 carburetor connections.
Overheating
Clear cooling passages and debris and avoid enclosed operation or excessive load.
